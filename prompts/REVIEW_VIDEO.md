# Review and improve a film

Inspect the current project, source records, contact sheet, full-size samples, and draft with narration. Read `docs/QUALITY_RUBRIC.md`, `docs/DATA_VISUALIZATION.md`, and the project storyboard. Review actual output, not only code.

Record findings in `renders/REVIEW.md` using timestamps and scene IDs. Rank them by factual integrity, broken rendering/audio, hierarchy, comprehension time, transitions, and polish. Explain what the viewer misunderstands or fails to see, then propose the smallest effective correction.

Score all rubric dimensions with evidence, calculate the weighted total, and flag any dimension below 3/5. The recommended target is 85/100. Do not infer factual validity from the presence of a citation: compare what appears on screen with the cited source, its period, unit, and qualification.

Inspect seams densely enough to see motion, and review the whole draft at playback speed. Check no narration is clipped, delayed, or replaced. Check important labels at realistic viewing size. For political comparisons, compare cropping, grading, scale, expression, and presentation time.

Unless asked for review only, implement the prioritized fixes, regenerate affected samples, re-inspect them, rerender the final, and run QA. Preserve a short before/after account. Return actual artifact paths, final scores, evidence for improvement, and unresolved issues. A plan to improve is not the completed revision.
