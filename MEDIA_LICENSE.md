To simplify reuse of the combined demonstration, the rendered demo and original graphic contributions are offered under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), with all above attribution retained. This does not affect the MIT framework code. Individual source assets retain their original licenses. Photography shows historical officeholders, explicitly labeled as 2024 context.

The revision 2 regional map, `hormuz-natural-earth-10m.geojson`, retains whole polygon components from Natural Earth's public-domain [1:10 million countries dataset](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson) intersecting the documented regional bounds. It is neither generated imagery nor 10-metre-resolution mapping. The pinned upstream revision, original and retained hashes, acquisition date, bounds and transformation are recorded in `projects/demo-fuel-prices/research/map-provenance.json`. `scripts/acquire-hormuz-map.mjs` reproduces the acquisition. The locator continues to use the credited world-atlas geometry.
