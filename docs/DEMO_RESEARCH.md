# Fuel-prices demonstration research

Retrieved and verified on **2026-10-03**. The demonstration explains mechanisms using dated evidence. It does not claim to report today's pump prices, today's shipping traffic, or the merits of an active political proposal.

## Institutional roles in Poland

**Source:** Constitution of the Republic of Poland, Articles 118–122, [official presidential archive: Chapter IV](https://www.prezydent.pl/kancelaria/archiwum/andrzej-duda/prawo/konstytucja-rp/iv-sejm-i-senat).

Verified points, paraphrased:

- Article 118: the Council of Ministers and President both have legislative initiative; they are not the only eligible initiators.
- Articles 119–121: bills go through the Sejm; the Senate can accept, amend, or reject an adopted bill within the constitutional procedure.
- Article 122: the President receives the adopted act for signature, with constitutional-review and reconsideration routes. A veto can be overridden by three-fifths of votes with the required quorum.

Suggested on-screen wording: **„Rząd / Prezydent → projekt ustawy → Sejm i Senat → decyzja Prezydenta”**, labeled **„schemat uproszczony”**. The branch matters: neither a speech nor a proposal changes a tax by itself. Do not imply that only the government may initiate a tax bill, or that signature is automatic.

**Tax-law source:** Article 217, [Rzecznik Praw Obywatelskich: Zasady nakładania podatków](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/pl/kategoria-konstytucyjna/art-217-zasady-nakladania-podatkow?page=2). It reserves the listed essential tax matters, including rates, to statute. Suggested concise documentary paraphrase: **„Podatki wymagają podstawy ustawowej”**. Label any typeset excerpt **„opracowanie na podstawie Konstytucji”**, not a screenshot or original scan.

Confidence: verified primary institutional sources. Visual use: balanced institutional comparison and a legal-document zoom. These records establish legal roles, not any particular person's present actions. A budget act follows special constitutional rules; do not substitute this ordinary-bill flow for a detailed budget procedure.

## Strait of Hormuz: a dated measure of scale

**Source:** U.S. Energy Information Administration, [Amid regional conflict, the Strait of Hormuz remains critical oil chokepoint](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=65504), published **2025-06-16**. EIA analysis uses Vortexa tanker tracking.

Supported numeric fact: during **2024**, oil flow through Hormuz averaged **20 million barrels per day**, approximately **20% of global petroleum-liquids consumption**. These are rounded values as reported in that article, not a measurement for 2026. The strait connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and Arabian Sea, between Iran and Oman.

Suggested on-screen wording: **„20 mln baryłek / dzień”**, subordinate **„Hormuz • średnia w 2024 r.”**, source **„EIA, 16.06.2025”**. If a share appears, use **„≈20% światowego zużycia paliw płynnych”**; do not relabel it as 20% of seaborne trade.

Confidence: verified dated agency estimate. Visual use: geographic zoom and route reveal. Animation of diminished traffic must be labeled **„scenariusz zakłócenia”** unless a matching observed time series is provided. Avoid presenting route particles as a measured tanker count or exact navigational tracks.

## Editorial limits for the demo

- An illustrative pump display must visibly say **„przykład”**; do not invent a live price.
- A quantified Polish retail-price composition needs an independently sourced, dated Polish dataset. Without that, use an unweighted mechanism diagram: crude and refined-product markets, currency, logistics, taxes, and retail costs. Do not manufacture component percentages.
- A proposed extraordinary-profit tax and redistribution chain is a policy proposal unless its exact adopted legal basis is supplied. It must not be depicted as an operating, guaranteed price-reduction mechanism.
- Institutional labels and symmetric visual treatment avoid unsupported assertions about current officeholders. If authentic portraits are added, preserve the image source, author, date, license, and equal compositional treatment.
- Keep these sources in project claim records and generate the final source list from those records. This document provides reviewed inputs; it does not replace project provenance.
