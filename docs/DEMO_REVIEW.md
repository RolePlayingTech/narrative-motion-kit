The revised film is [demo-fuel-prices-v2.mp4](../examples/demo-fuel-prices-v2.mp4): 19.6 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 FPS, six scenes and the preserved synthetic Polish narration. The source is `projects/demo-fuel-prices/project.json`. This is the implementation agent's editorial self-review, not independent publication approval. The revision responds to the user's concerns about map accuracy and visual finish. It replaces the regional map's coarse geography and weak contrast, enlarges principal elements, introduces contrasting paper pages, and repairs transitions. Future films select their own direction using [five presets and production guidance](ART_DIRECTION.md); this film's palette is not mandatory for other subjects.

## Known limits

Narration is still synthetic. Evidence and source labels merit longer holds in a full episode. Data and officeholder roles describe 2024, not current reporting. The map dataset's `10m` means **1:10 million scale**, not 10-metre accuracy. Route checks use that geometry and do not establish navigability or actual traffic. The route is visibly schematic, and the film does not prove that a Hormuz event caused the plotted Brent changes. Vertical samples are engineering coverage, not a completed 9:16 film.
