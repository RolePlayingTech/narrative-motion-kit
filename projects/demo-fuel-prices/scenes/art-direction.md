# Fuel-price demonstration — revision 2

Selected theme: `reportage`. The subject moves between public institutions, constitutional evidence, an industrial process, a geographic bottleneck and historical prices. Dark ink establishes the question and isolates authentic portraits; paper pages distinguish evidence, geography and data. Vermilion marks attention and routes, never a political affiliation. Mint is a subordinate process color. Both politicians receive the same image window, label style and reveal.

The film keeps the original 19.6-second narration and six semantic segments. The opening makes the example price dominant, the mechanism uses meaningful oil/refinery/ship/pump symbols, and the final chart completes at local 2.1 seconds for a longer reading hold. Changes in composition replace opaque pixels instead of fading both shots into grey.

The regional atlas uses genuine Natural Earth 1:10 million land polygons. Iran, Oman, the UAE, islands, gulf names, a north indicator and a scale establish the spatial relation. A global inset restores context. The route is explicitly schematic and checked against the local land geometry; no measured tanker count is implied. The 20% share and 20-million-barrel daily estimate retain the 2024 date and EIA source.

This is the direction chosen for this topic, not a default to impose on future films. Other projects should select a preset and composition grammar based on their own material.
